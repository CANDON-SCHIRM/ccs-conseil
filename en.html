Our mission

Make decisions with a clear understanding of the heritage.

CCS Conseil supports public and private owners facing complex decisions concerning heritage properties: historic monuments, protected buildings and built estates of significant cultural and regulatory value.

We work upstream and throughout projects to secure technical, regulatory and financial choices before they commit the heritage asset for decades to come.

Our areas of expertise

Independent expertise serving the property owner.

01 · ASSESSMENT

Heritage feasibility assessment

Preliminary strategic assessment covering the overall condition of the property, regulatory constraints, risks, indicative costs and intervention scenarios. The objective is to enable clear, informed decisions.

02 · PROJECT ASSISTANCE

Client-side project assistance

Support for the property owner in structuring, phasing and overseeing the project, liaising with architects, contractors and public heritage authorities.

03 · ADVISORY

Strategic advisory

Decision-making support in sensitive situations involving technical, regulatory or budgetary choices, emergencies or project deadlocks.

Who we work with

For those entrusted with the responsibility of heritage.

  • Private owners of listed or protected properties
  • Private owners of character properties
  • Local authorities and public institutions
  • Foundations, religious organisations and cultural institutions
  • Long-term heritage property investors
Our positioning

An independent trusted adviser.
